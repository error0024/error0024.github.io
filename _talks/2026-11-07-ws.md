Talk in the Signatures track of the 2nd Annual Post-Quantum Research Workshop, Cambridge, 10–12 October 2026. We discuss HG-WOTS, a one-time signature scheme that retains a significant security level even after key reuse, and how it can be applied to build few-time signature schemes for SPHINCS-like constructions.
