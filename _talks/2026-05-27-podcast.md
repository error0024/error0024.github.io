A Russian-language podcast episode on the fundamentals of post-quantum cryptography: how it differs from quantum cryptography, Shor's and Grover's algorithms and why public-key cryptography is the most exposed, the main families of post-quantum schemes (lattice-, hash- and code-based), hybrid signatures, the design of SPHINCS+, and the NIST standardization process. We also discuss "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks, the geopolitics of standards, migration of TLS and messengers, 2029 vs. 2035 timelines, and side-channel attacks.
