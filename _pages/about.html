I'm a cryptographer and I run Kudinov Cryptography Research. Most of my work is on hash-based and lattice-based cryptography, and on security proofs for these schemes, for example the FO transform for KEMs and proofs in the quantum random oracle model. I'm on the SPHINCS+ team (SLH-DSA). I consult for Blockstream, where I work on Bitcoin's post-quantum migration, including how hash-based and lattice-based signatures can be integrated into the protocol. I've also worked with the Ethereum Foundation on migration to post-quantum schemes. If you're a student or researcher working on similar problems, get in touch.
