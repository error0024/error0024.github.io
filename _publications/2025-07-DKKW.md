This note presents LeanSig, a new instantiation of the hash-based multi-signature framework for Ethereum's consensus layer. It uses a novel incomparable encoding, building on our CRYPTO 2025 work on hypercube encodings, that improves the trade-off between signature size and verification hashing, and explains how to apply these ideas within the original framework.
