We analyze the bit security of hash-based signatures in Lean4 and show that different ways of accounting for an adversary's work lead to different bit-security estimates. For two XMSS instances with 128-bit digests, we obtain about 127 bits of strong unforgeability under most accounting modes, but only 122 bits when counting only the adversary's direct random-oracle queries. We also prove a tight 127-bit bound for SLH-DSA-128s.
