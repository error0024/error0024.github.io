We review three lattice-based signature schemes — Dilithium, Falcon and Hawk — as candidate post-quantum replacements for Bitcoin's discrete-logarithm-based signatures, covering their intuition, technical background, performance and security. Hawk is included despite its withdrawal from NIST standardization, as its design remains of independent interest. We then assess deployment concerns specific to Bitcoin, including on-chain size, long-term security targets, implementation issues such as Falcon's floating-point signing, and wallet key derivation.
